The user page has a "Privacy and consent" section: each of the organization's current processing purposes as a switch, so an existing customer can reserve themselves against marketing. On means the organization may process for the purpose, so an unanswered reservation starts on and an unanswered consent starts off. A change is saved straight away, with a toast, and the switch moves back if saving fails. The section is left out when the organization has no purposes.
